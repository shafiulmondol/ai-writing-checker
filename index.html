Model / method
Primary detector: TMR (Target Mining RoBERTa), an English AI-text detector trained on the RAID benchmark using Focal Loss and Self-Hard-Negative mining. The published model reports strong RAID benchmark results and is specifically designed to reduce false positives. This browser build uses its ONNX conversion.

Aggregation: The document is split into overlapping prose segments of a few hundred words. Each segment receives an AI probability from the detector; overlapping segment predictions are combined and mapped back to sentences. The document score is a word-weighted aggregate of the segment/sentence evidence. This follows the general research pattern of contextual segments + sentence-level evidence used by academic detectors, but it is not Turnitin's proprietary implementation.
Important limitation
AI detection is probabilistic and cannot prove authorship. A detector can misclassify human writing, especially short, edited, non-native, or out-of-domain English. Scores below 20% are shown as “low-confidence / not surfaced” rather than pretending that a tiny numerical difference is meaningful. For reliable evaluation, use at least 300 words of continuous prose.
