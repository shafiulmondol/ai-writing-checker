Model / method
Uses the TMR AI-text classifier through its ONNX model with Transformers.js directly in the browser. Long documents are processed in overlapping windows. Sentence scores are aggregated from the windows containing each sentence.
Important limitation
This is a probabilistic detector, not proof of authorship. Very short text is not reliable for AI detection, so the app refuses to produce an AI percentage below the minimum evidence threshold. The selected model is primarily trained for English and can produce false positives, especially on short or out-of-domain text.
